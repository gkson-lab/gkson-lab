about me

how did we get here?

honestly, by saying yes to whatever looked interesting that week.

i'm gk. gk-labs is where everything i make ends up: the useful stuff, the useless stuff, and a lot that started as one and turned into the other.

right now that means an open source ai called onyx, a 150 tool app it lives inside, a task manager that tells you more than it probably should, and helping out on oreon and its security side.

most of it is javascript, some of it is python, and all of it gets written on windows.
